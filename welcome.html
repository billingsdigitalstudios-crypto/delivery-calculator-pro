Use Dynamic Shortcodes for One-Click Script Spawning

When crafting your message layouts in the settings view, you can place these dynamic text placeholders anywhere. The extension replaces them with automated routing telemetry variables on every calculation run:

• {fee} → The computed delivery cost payout summary
• {miles} → The spatial road distance calculation output
• {duration} → Google Maps' drive time duration metric
• {payment} → Your saved cash handling username handle
